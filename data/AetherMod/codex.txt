AETHERMOD YHWH BIBLIO CODEX DATASET
=================================
This is the offline text-corpus gateway for the Codex reader.
The binary contains the book index and navigation shell; a complete public-domain
or licensed corpus may be placed here or in this directory without changing the
application. The reader never requires a network connection.
