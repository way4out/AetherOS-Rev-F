CORPUS RULE
-----------
The application provides the reader, index, search/navigation shell and name-layer mapping.
Full text is loaded from public-domain or properly licensed UTF-8 corpus files placed under
SD:/data/AetherMod/. Modern copyrighted translations are not silently bundled.

FILE CONTRACT
-------------
Recommended: one UTF-8 TXT file per book.
